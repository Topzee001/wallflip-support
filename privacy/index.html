WallFlip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026. Applies to WallFlip 1.0, published by Topzee. Contact: topzee001@gmail.com.

Photos and Local Data

WallFlip accesses only images selected through Android's Photo Picker. It saves resized JPEG copies in private app storage for previews and wallpaper rotation. Original images are not modified. Image names, order, rotation interval, screen target and last-change information are stored locally. WallFlip passes images to Android's wallpaper system to apply your chosen wallpapers. It does not upload photos or settings.

V1 has no accounts, cloud sync, online wallpaper feed, advertising, analytics, tracking SDKs, purchase processing, or developer-operated backend. The release does not request Internet access. Android's Photo Picker may offer cloud images through services you use; those services follow their own privacy policies.

Permissions and Background Activity

WallFlip uses Android's wallpaper permission and WorkManager for the schedule you enable. Android manages background jobs, wake locks, scheduling-library network-state checks, and restart restoration. V1 does not request full-library photo access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, notifications, accessibility services, or exact-alarm access. It does not monitor screen events.

Retention and Deletion

Private copies and settings remain until you remove them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Removing an image deletes only its private copy, not the original. Clearing storage or uninstalling deletes WallFlip's data. A wallpaper already applied to Android may remain afterward. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. There is no server account or photo collection.

Support and This Website

If you email support, the developer and email provider process your address, message, and attachments to respond. Avoid sending sensitive information or private photos unnecessarily. Support emails are kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Email topzee001@gmail.com to request deletion of your support correspondence.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process IP addresses and technical logs under its privacy statement. The website adds no analytics, cookies, tracking scripts, or embedded ads. Website hosting and email correspondence are separate from the offline app.

Audience and Changes

WallFlip is a general-purpose personalisation tool intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and over. It does not ask for your date of birth, publish users' photos or operate social features. Contact support with privacy questions. Material changes will be reflected in this policy and, where needed, the app and Play disclosures. Future features are not included in these V1 disclosures.
