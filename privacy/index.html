WallFlip Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026. Applies to WallFlip 1.0, published by Topzee. Contact: topzee001@gmail.com.

Photos and Local Data

WallFlip accesses only images selected through Android's Photo Picker. It saves resized JPEG copies in private app storage for previews and wallpaper rotation. Original images are not modified. Image names, order, rotation interval, screen target and last-change information are stored locally. WallFlip passes images to Android's wallpaper system to apply your chosen wallpapers. It does not upload photos or settings.

V1 has no accounts, cloud sync, online wallpaper feed, advertising or purchases. Your wallpaper rotation works offline. Android's Photo Picker may offer cloud images through services you use; those services follow their own privacy policies.

App Updates and Technical Data

WallFlip uses Internet access for software updates. Shorebird checks for fixes when the app starts and may download a signed patch for a later launch. It receives a random installation identifier, app/release and patch versions, platform, processor architecture, update events and technical failure details. These support update delivery, staged distribution, install counts and reliability monitoring. The identifier belongs to this app installation, not your advertising ID. Wallpaper photos, names and rotation settings are not sent to Shorebird.

These automatic checks have no in-app opt-out. Update requests use HTTPS; service infrastructure can also process IP addresses and network logs. Shorebird and its providers may process records outside your country and retain them as described in the Shorebird privacy policy. See Shorebird's technical data description.

WallFlip also asks Google Play whether a newer store release is available. Google's update library handles device metadata, the app version and installed module/asset-pack information to offer and install updates. Google documents encrypted processing and a fixed retention period in its in-app update data disclosure. WallFlip has no advertising or cross-app tracking, and does not send wallpaper content to either update service.

Permissions and Background Activity

WallFlip uses Android's wallpaper permission and WorkManager for the schedule you enable. Android manages background jobs, wake locks, scheduling-library network-state checks, and restart restoration. For Every day at midnight, WallFlip sets a single alarm for the next local midnight. If you allow Alarms & reminders, Android can deliver a precise alarm. Applying the wallpaper can still be delayed by device restrictions or a busy device. The permission only wakes WallFlip to change your wallpaper and you can turn it off at any time. V1 does not request full-library photo access, location, contacts, camera, microphone, notifications or accessibility services. It does not monitor screen events.

Retention and Deletion

Private copies and settings remain until you remove them, clear app storage, or uninstall. Removing an image deletes only its private copy, not the original. Clearing storage or uninstalling deletes WallFlip's data. A wallpaper already applied to Android may remain afterward. App data is excluded from Android cloud backup and device transfer. There is no server account or photo collection. Clearing storage or uninstalling resets Shorebird's local installation identifier; it does not automatically delete technical records already held by update providers. Contact topzee001@gmail.com about data requests; we can coordinate with the relevant provider where records can be identified.

Support and This Website

If you email support, the developer and email provider process your address, message, and attachments to respond. Avoid sending sensitive information or private photos unnecessarily. Support emails are kept only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Email topzee001@gmail.com to request deletion of your support correspondence.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process IP addresses and technical logs under its privacy statement. The website adds no analytics, cookies, tracking scripts, or embedded ads. Website hosting and email correspondence are separate from app update requests.

Audience and Changes

WallFlip is a general-purpose personalisation tool intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and over. It does not ask for your date of birth, publish users' photos or operate social features. Contact support with privacy questions. Material changes will be reflected in this policy and, where needed, the app and Play disclosures. Future features are not included in these V1 disclosures.
