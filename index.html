Scheduled changes are delayed

Android schedules background work with battery usage in mind. Intervals are approximate, not exact alarms. Check that Scheduled rotation is on and your collection contains images.

Open Setup, then App battery settings. On Samsung, check that WallFlip is not in Sleeping apps or Deep sleeping apps. Force-stopping pauses background work until you open the app again.

Adding or removing photos

In Images, tap Add wallpapers and select images through Android's Photo Picker. Tap an image to preview. Long-press and drag to reorder. Removing an image deletes only WallFlip's copy, not your original.

Home and lock screens

Choose Home, Lock, or Both in Settings. Both applies the same image to both screens. Device and launcher behaviour may vary.

Your privacy

Photos and settings stay locally in the app. V1 has no accounts, ads, analytics, or payments. Read the privacy policy.

Reporting a problem

Include phone model, Android version, WallFlip version, and what happened. Avoid sending private photos or sensitive information unnecessarily.
